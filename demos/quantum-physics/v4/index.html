QUANTUM!

The Astonishing Adventures of ELI the ELECTRON

He's smaller than small. He breaks every rule of reality. And he is about to ruin your common sense — one panel at a time.

SCROLL TO BEGIN ↓
CH. 1TWO-FACED!
Name's Eli. Am I a particle? A wave? …Yes.
Our hero travels like a wave — spread out, rippling, everywhere at once…
ZWOOM!
…then lands like a dot. One spot. Bam.
"WAVE OR PARTICLE?" IS THE WRONG QUESTION, PAL.
Ask about my path and I act like a wave. Ask where I hit and I'm a single point. Two faces, no contradiction.
CH. 2THE IMPOSSIBLE TRICK
They fired me at a wall with two thin slits. One of me. At a time.
So naturally… I went through BOTH.
BLISH!
Fire a thousand of me and the dots stack into stripes — bright, dark, bright, dark.
That's interference. I lined up… with myself.
But the second you peek to see which slit I used…
POOF!
…the stripes vanish. Looking changes the story.
CH. 3BOTH! AT! ONCE!
Before you check, I'm not a 0. I'm not a 1. I'm both, blended.
SHIMMER~
It isn't "we don't know yet." The blend is real. I'm genuinely living every option.
THEN YOU OPEN THE BOX…
…and I SLAM into a single answer.
CH. 4CAN'T PIN ME DOWN
Wanna know exactly where I am and exactly how fast I'm going?
Yeah… about that.
WOBBLE
Pin my position and my speed goes wild. Pin my speed and my position smears out.
It's not bad equipment. It's the law — Heisenberg's uncertainty principle. Squeeze one, the other explodes.
CH. 5JUMPING THE STEPS
Round an atom I can't hover just anywhere. Only certain rungs.
Jump DOWN a rung and I pay the difference…
PING!
…by spitting out a photon of one exact color. That's why neon signs glow and stars have barcodes of light.
CH. 6THE SPOOKY TWIN
I've got a twin. Born linked. Now we're galaxies apart.
Measure me UP… and instantly my twin is DOWN. No signal. No delay.
SPOOKY ACTION AT A DISTANCE!
— some guy named Einstein, who hated this
Real, though. Proven. But — we still can't text with it. Sorry.
CH. 7WALKIN' THROUGH WALLS
A wall too tall to climb? A barrier I've got no business crossing?
Watch this.
BLIP!
…and I'm on the other side.
No, I didn't break the wall. My wave leaks a little way in, so there's a real chance I just appear beyond it. It's how the Sun burns and how a USB stick remembers your homework.
CH. 8SPLIT DECISION
Shoot me through a lopsided magnet. You'd expect a smeared smudge, right?
Nope. I split. Clean. Two sharp dots. Up… or down. Nothing between.
SHK-SHK!
My "spin" only ever gives two answers. Quantized, baby.
CH. 9THE MOMENT YOU LOOK
Here's the secret hiding under everything you just read…
Until you look, I'm a fog of maybe. A cloud of every possibility at once.
SNAP!
The instant you measure me… the whole cloud freezes into one fact.
The math nails the odds perfectly. But why you only ever see one outcome? Even the greatest minds are still arguing. That's not a gap in you — it's the honest edge of reality.

THE END?

Nah. Every time you look… it all starts again.

You just scrolled through wave-particle duality, the double slit, superposition, uncertainty, quantization, entanglement, tunneling, spin, and measurement — the nine weirdest rules in the universe. Not bad for a comic.
