Lab 05 // rungs, not ramps

Energy comes in steps.
The in-between is forbidden.

An electron bound to an atom cannot have just any energy. It is stuck on a ladder of allowed rungs. To move up it must absorb exactly the right amount of light, and when it drops back down it spits that energy out as a photon of one precise color. That is why each element glows in its own fixed set of lines, a barcode written in light. Click the bars below and watch the jump that makes each one.

The word "quantum" is born right here: a fixed, smallest chunk.

Max Planck found in 1900 that light energy only comes in discrete packets, and Bohr realized the same was true of electron orbits. The whole field is named after this one fact. The colors a neon sign glows, the light a star throws across the galaxy, the way a laser holds one pure tone, all of it is electrons hopping between fixed rungs and paying the difference in photons.
