How to use this

You are not supposed to "get it" on the first read. You are supposed to play with it.

Quantum physics feels strange because our intuition was built by throwing rocks and watching them land, not by watching electrons. Every lab here turns one weird rule into a knob you can turn. Twist it past the sensible range. Break it. The point where it stops making sense is exactly where the physics lives.
